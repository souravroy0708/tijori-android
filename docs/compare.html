Pick Tijori if

you mostly use your phone, want your Gmail and everyday passwords in one place for free, and want them back after an uninstall without trusting a password company’s server.

Pick Bitwarden or 1Password if

you need the same vault on a laptop and a phone every day, or want breach alerts and passkeys today. They sync through their own servers.

Google Password Manager is fine if

you’re happy keeping passwords inside your Google account. Its weak spot is that one Google account holds both your email and the keys to everything else.

What we took from the paid apps

Features that people pay for elsewhere, now in Tijori for free.

  • Automatic backup that survives uninstallLike a cloud vault, but the copy sits in your own Google Drive and Tijori still never touches the internet.
  • One-step importFrom Google Password Manager, Chrome, Bitwarden, 1Password, LastPass and Firefox, with app logins linked for autofill.
  • Password historyThe last 5 passwords of every login, as in Bitwarden and 1Password.
  • One-tap copy from the listNo need to open the item; the clipboard clears itself.
  • Passphrase-only restoreSimpler than 1Password’s Secret Key, with the stricter two-secret mode one switch away.
