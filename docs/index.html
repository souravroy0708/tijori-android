Google Drive backup

Uninstall it. Lose the phone. Your passwords come back.

Most offline vaults lose everything when the app is removed. Tijori keeps one encrypted file in your Google Drive and updates it a few seconds after every change. You set it up once, during setup.

  1. Save as usual

    Add your Gmail, import the rest from Google Password Manager. Each change is copied to Drive, already locked.

  2. Something happens

    You uninstall Tijori, reset the phone, or the phone is lost or stolen. The copy on the phone is gone.

  3. Install again

    Install Tijori, tap Restore from Google Drive, open Tijori-backup.tijori from Drive.

  4. Everything is back

    Type your master passphrase. Every password returns, and backups carry on into the same file.

Google stores the file but can’t read it: titles, usernames and passwords are all encrypted on your phone first. Tijori itself has no internet permission; the Google Drive app carries the locked file. Read how it works.

Questions people ask first

More in the help guide, or write to tosourav6@gmail.com.

If I uninstall Tijori, are my passwords gone?

Not if Drive backup is on (Tijori asks during setup). The backup file stays in your Google Drive. Install Tijori again, tap Restore from Google Drive, pick the file and type your passphrase.

What do I need to restore?

Your master passphrase. That’s all, with “Restore with passphrase only” on, which is the default. If you switch it off for maximum protection, you’ll also need the Secret Key printed on your Emergency Kit.

Can Google read my passwords in Drive?

No. The file is encrypted on your phone before the Drive app ever sees it. Without your passphrase it’s random-looking data.

What if I forget my passphrase?

Nobody can reset it, not even us. That’s what makes the vault safe. Write it on paper and keep it at home. If you made nominee cards, your family can still open the Family room.

Can I move my passwords from Google Password Manager?

Yes. Export them from Google Password Manager (⚙ Settings → Export passwords), then in Tijori go to Settings → Import passwords. Delete the exported file afterwards; it isn’t encrypted.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, completely. Tijori never needs the internet. The Drive copy updates whenever the Google Drive app is online.

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no premium tier, no account.
