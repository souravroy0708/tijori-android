Privacy policy

Short version: Tijori collects nothing. Your passwords are encrypted on your phone, and the only copy that leaves it is the encrypted backup you choose to put in your own Google Drive.

Who we are

Tijori is made by Sourav Roy in Hyderabad, India. Contact: tosourav6@gmail.com. Last updated: 20 November 2026.

What Tijori collects

Nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything to us or anyone else. There are no accounts, analytics, ads, advertising IDs, crash-reporting services or third-party SDKs.

What stays on your phone

  • Your vault: every item (title, usernames, passwords, notes) is encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The keys come from your master passphrase (Argon2id) and a Secret Key held in the phone’s secure hardware.
  • Settings such as language and theme, and a short activity log (for example “unlocked”, “backed up”) that never contains titles or secrets.
  • Android’s own cloud backup and device-transfer are switched off for Tijori.

Your Google Drive backup

If you turn on Google Drive backup, Tijori writes one encrypted file to a place you pick using Android’s standard file picker. The Google Drive app on your phone uploads it to your own Drive account. Google receives only encrypted data and cannot read your passwords. We never receive the file.

With “Restore with passphrase only” on, the file also contains your Secret Key, sealed by a key stretched from your passphrase. That lets your passphrase alone restore the backup, and it means the file’s safety depends on your passphrase being strong. You can switch this off at any time; then restoring needs both your passphrase and the Secret Key from your Emergency Kit.

Files you create

Extra backup copies, Emergency Kit PDFs, nominee cards and Family packages are saved only where you choose. Imported password files (CSV) are read on the phone and never stored by Tijori; delete them after importing.

Crash reports

If Tijori crashes, a report without any vault data is kept on the phone. Next time, the app shows it and lets you copy it yourself. Nothing is sent automatically.

Deleting your data

Settings → Erase this vault, or uninstall the app, removes everything on the phone. Delete Tijori-backup.tijori from your Google Drive to remove the backup. There is no copy anywhere else to delete.

Children

Tijori is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

This website

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other companies. The passphrase demo runs only in your browser.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date. Tijori will never start collecting data silently: that would require adding the internet permission, which the build check rejects.
